Models
------

The models embedded in the Resources/models folder of the plugin bundle are
developed by LAION (https://laion.ai/) and converted to the GGUF format
(with quantization for the q8_0 and q4_k_m variants) by the Clap.cpp project
(https://huggingface.co/gorse-io). They are distributed under the Apache
License 2.0 (Apache-2.0.txt).

- larger_clap_general (q8_0 and q4_k_m)
  https://huggingface.co/laion/larger_clap_general
- larger_clap_music_and_speech (q8_0 and q4_k_m)
  https://huggingface.co/laion/larger_clap_music_and_speech
- clap2-small-audioxl-s1B (f16)
  https://huggingface.co/laion/clap2-small-audioxl-s1B
- clap2-xxs-audioxl-s1B (f16)
  https://huggingface.co/laion/clap2-xxs-audioxl-s1B
